# COROS Training Plan Summary

**Plan period:** November 16, 2026-January 23, 2027  
**Goal event:** 100k Ultra on January 23, 2027  
**Scheduled running before race day:** 439 miles  
**Strength work:** 14 sessions (full-body early in the plan, then running-specific bodyweight work)

This revised schedule keeps the November 16 start date and compresses the final build to a 70-mile peak week before the event. Most mileage is aerobic-endurance running, supported by tempo, threshold, progression, or alternating-effort workouts. The peak concludes with back-to-back 22- and 20-mile runs; race week then reduces volume to 17 easy miles, with no long run or strength work.

## Training progression

1. **Foundation (Nov 16-Dec 13):** Establishes aerobic volume at 33-46 miles per week, includes full-body strength, and uses 12-15-mile long runs.
2. **Build (Dec 14-Jan 3):** Raises volume from 49 to 59 miles per week, transitions to running-specific bodyweight work, and extends long runs to 20 miles.
3. **Final long-run block (Jan 4-17):** Uses a 38-mile recovery week before a 70-mile peak with back-to-back 22- and 20-mile aerobic runs.
4. **Taper and race (Jan 18-23):** Reduces running to 17 easy miles before the January 23 100k. Follow the nutrition strategy and remain in the aerobic-endurance zone for most of the event.
